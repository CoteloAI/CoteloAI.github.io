How it works

Three steps between a vague request and the right result.

  1. 1

    Understand

    Cotelo reads the request the way a person would: brand, category, color, size, price and intent, even when it's phrased loosely.

  2. 2

    Intelligent Semantic Match

    Users don't need the right keywords or terminology. Cotelo matches meaning against your live catalog.

  3. 3

    Rank

    Items that meet the most of what was asked come first.
