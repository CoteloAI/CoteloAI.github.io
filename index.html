How it works

Three steps between an event brief and a plan you can act on.

  1. 1

    Brief

    Describe your event in plain words: topic, audience, size, dates, budget and brand. Cotelo turns it into clear requirements.

  2. 2

    Review and approve

    Cotelo shows a ranked shortlist of sponsors. Compare options, approve what fits, and change what does not.

  3. 3

    Share in one place

    Cotelo publishes your approved sponsors on one page. Everything is together, so no one collects scattered information.
